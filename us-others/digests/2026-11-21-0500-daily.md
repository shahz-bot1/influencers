**Joseph Hogue, CFA: 1 video.** "ONE ETF Changes How You Invest for Income" (published 20 Nov). A review of IQQI, the new Quantify Funds Income Q Nasdaq 100 Premium Income ETF (video sponsored by Quantify Funds). The fund gets Nasdaq 100 exposure via purchased in-the-money call options and generates income by selling puts/put spreads on individual stocks and the index, aiming for weekly distributions and to outperform the Nasdaq 100 over a full cycle. He likes selling puts instead of covered calls because it keeps the upside uncapped, and explains why individual-stock puts pay higher premiums than index puts — single names are more volatile, and concentrated holders buy puts for protection. Risks: in a correction the purchased calls and short puts can lose money at the same time; short-put notional is capped at 100% of net assets but total exposure isn't; individual-stock and active-manager risk; no track record. Lukewarm on weekly distributions.
- IQQI — CONDITIONAL_BUY (first tracked). Worth considering for investors who want tech growth plus recurring income (e.g. approaching retirement), as a diversifier alongside non-Nasdaq holdings — never as all of your money. "A better long-term bullish strategy than limiting your upside by selling calls."
